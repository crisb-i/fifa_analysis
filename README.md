This is a break down of FIFA teams and their likelihood of winning tournaments in R. 
Simulations were ran to get an idea of which team would be best surprise was belgium being so high. 
Brazil made sense since historically they won many but as you saw in the last world cup that did not work out.
I also wanted to see which variables had the highest correlation for wins.
It was the rankings, a lower rank gave the higher likelihood of winning.
This seemed foreseen since rankings take in most of the variables listed.
